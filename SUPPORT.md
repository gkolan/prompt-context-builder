# Support

Before reporting a problem, capture:

- the product version or source revision;
- the entry point: record page, Flow, Apex, preview, publish, or health check;
- the stable reason code and correlation ID;
- whether the failure affects one record, one template, or every request; and
- the relevant local, deployment, or Apex test result.

Do not include generated prompts, customer field values, session IDs, access tokens, passwords,
generated SOQL, bind values, or raw production debug logs in a public report.

For common causes, see [Frequently asked questions](./docs/faqs/README.md),
[Troubleshoot generation](./docs/diagnostics/README.md), and
[Production operations](./docs/production-operations/README.md). Report security-sensitive issues
privately according to the [Security policy](./.github/SECURITY.md), not through a public issue.
